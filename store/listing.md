# App Store listing: Last App

Written for App Store search (ASO) around preppers, doomsday prep, survival and how-to guides.

## Name (30 characters max)

Apple caps the name at 30 characters. "Last App - Prepper's Survival Guide" is 35, so it gets split across the name and subtitle. Both are searched, so every word still counts.

- **Name:** `Last App - Survival Guide` (25)
- **Subtitle:** `Offline Prepper & SHTF How-To` (29)
- Alternative name if "prepper" must be in the name itself: `Last App: Prepper Survival` (26), with subtitle `Offline Doomsday How-To Guide` (29)
- Name under the icon on the phone stays `Last App`.

## Keywords (100 characters max, 99 used)

```
doomsday,prepping,emergency,first aid,bug out,bushcraft,disaster,off grid,wilderness,apocalypse,kit
```

Words already in the name and subtitle (survival, guide, prepper, offline, how-to, SHTF) are left out on purpose. Apple counts them already, and repeating them wastes space.

## Promotional text (170 max)

No power. No signal. No problem. Step-by-step survival help, first aid you can follow out loud, and a full library of how-to guides, all saved on your phone.

## Description

When the power is out and the phone lines are down, Last App still works.

Last App is a complete offline survival guide. Download it once and everything is saved on your phone: emergency first aid, disaster plans, prepper checklists and a full library of how-to guides. No internet, no signal and no account needed.

KNOW WHAT TO DO IN SECONDS
- 10 emergency cards with big, plain steps: bleeding, CPR, choking, burns, broken bones, overheating, hypothermia, safe water, lost outdoors and power outages
- "Show me" mode: moving pictures, one step at a time, read out loud
- Built-in timers and a CPR beat you can follow
- Written for when help can't come, in words anyone can follow

READY FOR YOUR DISASTERS
- Hurricanes, tornadoes, earthquakes, wildfires, floods, winter storms and extreme heat
- What to do before, during and after
- Advice that fits where you live and who is in your home

PREP LIKE A PRO
- Checklists for 3 days, 2 weeks and 1 year
- Water, food and supplies sized for your household
- A plain reason next to every item, so you know why it matters

HOW-TO GUIDES THAT NEED NO POWER
- First aid and health, safe water, food and gardening, shelter and warmth, power and repairs, radios, off-grid living and hand-tool DIY
- Every guide is picked to work with no electricity, no internet and no special equipment
- Army and FEMA field manuals
- Search everything, or just ask a question and get an answer from your guides
- Find words inside any guide, jump between sections, and make the text bigger

OFFLINE MAPS
- Save the area around you and use it with no signal
- Find hospitals, shelters, police, fire stations, water and fuel near you

FULLY OFFLINE, FULLY PRIVATE
- Works in airplane mode
- No account and no ads
- Your location stays on your phone

Start free with the Small pack: the essentials for first aid, water, fire and shelter. Go bigger with the Medium or Large pack, each a one-time purchase. Own Medium? Upgrade to Large for the difference. No subscription, ever.

Whether you are a seasoned prepper, getting ready for storm season, heading off-grid, or just want your family to be ready for anything, Last App is the one app to have when everything else stops working.

Last App gives general information and is not medical advice or a replacement for professional help. If you can reach emergency services, do that first.
