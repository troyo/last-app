# Safety review of the built-in cards (2026-11-18)

Reviewed: src/lib/quick-help.ts (all 23 cards) and the matching Show me lines in src/lib/show-me.ts.
Not a substitute for a medical professional, who should still read the cards and the Show me pictures before launch.

## Must fix

1. **water card + Show me water step: bleach strength.** Drops depend on the bleach. Say: "Check the label: 6% bleach, 8 drops per gallon. 8.25% bleach, 6 drops. Double it if the water is cloudy." (EPA guidance.)
2. **store-water, water heater step: scald and fire risk.** Add "Let it cool for a few hours first" and "Don't turn its power or gas back on until the tank is full again" (an empty heater can burn out or start a fire).
3. **find-water step 1.** Toilet tank only "if there's no blue cleaner or tablet in it". Water heater: "let it cool first".
4. **power card contradicts food-cold.** Power says fridge food warm "more than 4 hours", food-cold says "more than 2 hours". Make power read: "Throw out meat, milk, eggs and leftovers once the fridge has been off more than 4 hours and they're no longer cold."
5. **light card "know" overpromises.** "do not flare up if the jar tips" → "burn slowly and are much safer than fuel. A tipped lamp can still start a fire, so keep it on a plate."

## Should add

6. **cpr:** when to stop with no help coming: "If there's no sign of life after 30 minutes of pushing, it is okay to stop. You did everything you could." Add a child line: "Child 1 to 8: one hand, about 2 inches deep."
7. **choking know:** "Pregnant or very large? Push on the middle of the chest instead of the belly."
8. **cold dont:** frostbite: "Don't thaw frozen fingers or toes if they might freeze again. Don't rub them."
9. **cook next (pot wrapped in towels):** "Eat it within 2 hours, or bring it back to a boil first."
10. **fire dont:** "Don't light one in dry, windy weather near grass or brush. It can turn into a wildfire."
11. **snake-bite know:** "In Australia, wrap the whole limb firmly with a bandage from the bite outward, then splint it. Elsewhere, don't wrap it tight." (Can use the place setting.)
12. **bleeding step 5 + Show me "Raise their legs":** Red Cross no longer routinely recommends raising the legs. Low risk; "Lay them down and keep them warm" is enough.

## Looked fine

CPR depth and rate, choking sequence, burns (20 min cool, no ice/butter), broken bone, overheating (incl. the rehydration recipe, matches WHO), too cold, lost outdoors, shelter, signal (3s, SOS, CB 9, marine 16), stay warm, snake bite (no cutting, sucking, ice or tourniquet), toilet (200 ft from water), food-cold timings (USDA), stay cool, phone, save water.
