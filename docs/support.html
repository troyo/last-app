Support

Need help with Last App? Here are answers to the common questions.

I bought a pack on another phone. How do I get it back?

Open More → My packs and tap "I already paid. Restore my purchase". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, and connected to the internet for a moment.

I own Medium. Do I pay full price for Large?

No. If you own Medium, Large is offered as a lower-priced upgrade, so nobody pays more than the Large price in total.

Does it really work with no signal?

Yes. Once a pack and your maps are saved, everything works in airplane mode. Emergency cards, disaster guides and checklists are built in and work from the first launch.

A download stopped halfway.

Nothing is lost. Open My packs and tap "Finish saving" when you have internet again. It picks up where it left off.

Something else?

Report a problem or ask a question on our help page.
