Privacy Policy

Last App: Prepper's Survival Guide · Effective November 18, 2026

Short version: Last App does not collect, store or sell any personal information. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you read, save or set up stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your place and household. The area you live in and who is in your home (used to fit guides and checklists to you) are saved only on your device.
  • Your location. If you allow it, your phone's location is used only to show where you are on the map and to suggest your area. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • What you read and save. Guides, maps, checklists, searches and questions you ask the app are handled entirely on your device.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

When the app uses the internet

Last App is built to work offline. It only goes online when you choose to download something:

  • Guide packs are downloaded from GitHub (github.com), which hosts the files.
  • Offline maps are downloaded from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org).

Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and basic request details when you download from them. We don't receive this information. See the GitHub privacy statement and OpenFreeMap for how they handle it.

Purchases

The Medium and Large packs are bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google handle your payment and account details under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, email or card. The app only learns which packs your account owns, and saves that on your phone so they stay unlocked offline.

Children

Last App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
